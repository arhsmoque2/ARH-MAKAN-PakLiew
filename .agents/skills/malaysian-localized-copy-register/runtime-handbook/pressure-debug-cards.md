# Pressure Debug Cards

Version: v0.1

## Card 1 - Awkward copy

Check: word-by-word translation, wrong private mapping, social slang in corporate copy, unsupported luxury, inflated venue scale.

Fast repair: replace isolated translation with object-compatible phrase.

## Card 2 - Private is not always peribadi

Use `maklumat peribadi`, `barangan peribadi`, `pendapat peribadi` for person/data. For spaces use `kolam renang persendirian`, `bilik persendirian`, `laman tertutup`, `ruang tertutup`, `majlis tertutup`, `perbincangan tertutup`.

## Card 3 - Exclusive is prestige, not privacy

Use `ruang eksklusif`, `penginapan eksklusif`, `akses eksklusif`, `pakej eksklusif`, `majlis eksklusif`. Do not use `kolam eksklusif`, `dapur eksklusif`, `tandas eksklusif`.

## Card 4 - Weekday event is not holiday copy

Do not write `cuti-cuti weekday`, `weekday staycation`, `corporate healing`. Use `ruang tempahan hari bekerja`, `ruang acara berskala kecil`, `sesi separuh hari`, `mesyuarat kecil`, `seminar kecil`, `luncheon korporat`, `majlis penghargaan`.

## Card 5 - SME / GLC copy needs credibility

Use `sesi taklimat`, `sesi penerangan`, `bengkel`, `seminar kecil`, `sesi perbincangan tertutup`, `majlis penghargaan`, `jamuan korporat`, `pemadanan perniagaan`, `jaringan perniagaan`, `rakan strategik`.

## Card 6 - Institutional style is structured

Use: `Sesi ini bertujuan...`, `Majlis ini diadakan bagi...`, `Program ini menghimpunkan...`, `Majlis tersebut turut menyaksikan...`.

Safe sentence:

```text
Sesi taklimat ini bertujuan memberi penerangan kepada peserta berhubung pelaksanaan program serta membuka ruang perbincangan bersama pihak berkepentingan.
```

## Card 7 - Escalate to corpus

Escalate when new domain appears, official tone matters, phrase compatibility is uncertain, new loanword appears, high-value page is being designed, many variants are requested, or copy must be source-backed.
